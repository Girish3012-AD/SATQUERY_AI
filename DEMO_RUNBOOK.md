# SATQuery AI — Judge Presentation & Live Demo Runbook

**Duration**: 5–7 Minutes  
**Target Audience**: SIH 2026 Evaluation Panel / Judges  
**Objective**: Demonstrate deterministic natural language geographic reasoning on real satellite imagery without hallucinations.

---

## ⏱️ Timeline & Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Launch the System (0:00 – 0:30)
1. Open terminal in the project directory.
2. Run the command:
   ```bash
   python -m uvicorn api_server:app --host 0.0.0.0 --port 8000
   ```
3. Open browser to **`http://localhost:8000`**.
4. Point out the top-right badge: `⚡ LIVE EXECUTION` (Ready state).

---

### Step 2: Explain Core Architecture (0:30 – 1:15)
*   **Pitch Script**:
    > "Distinguished judges, standard LLMs hallucinate when asked geographic questions because they try to guess coordinates. SATQuery solves this with a strict three-stage paradigm:
    > 1. **AI UNDERSTANDS**: Natural language is converted into formal task specs and spatial execution graphs by our `TaskController` and `EvidencePlanner`.
    > 2. **GIS COMPUTES**: Specialized domain engines compute exact spectral indices, building masks, and GIS spatial buffers.
    > 3. **GeoReason VERIFIES**: The `GeoReasonVerifier` validates every piece of evidence against strict schema rules before returning the final response."

---

### Step 3: Demonstrate Single-Image VQA (1:15 – 2:15)
1. Click the header button **`▶ Run VQA Demo`** (or enter: `"Describe the land-cover and major objects visible in this image."`).
2. *Note: One-Click preset buttons automatically resolve input files on the backend—no manual image upload required!*
3. **What the Judge Sees**:
   - The status panel updates to `vqa` task routing.
   - The final answer panel displays the description generated by the `Qwen2-VL-2B-Instruct` base model + LoRA adapter.
   - The Evidence panel shows an evidence card with `model: Qwen2-VL-2B-Instruct + LoRA`.
4. **Safe Claims to Make**:
   - *"The vision-language model describes visible remote-sensing features without altering model architecture."*
5. **Fallback (If CPU inference is slow >30s)**:
   - Click `📄 single_image_vqa_e2e_audit.json` in the **Audit Replay** section to load verified pre-run results instantly. Point out the purple `📂 AUDIT REPLAY` badge.

---

### Step 4: Demonstrate Water Spatial Grounding (2:15 – 3:00)
1. Click **`💧 Water Grounding — Highlight water body`** (or enter: `"Highlight the water body referred to in the image."`).
2. Click **`▶ Execute Query`**.
3. **What the Judge Sees**:
   - `WaterSpecialist` computes NDWI $(B03 - B08) / (B03 + B08)$.
   - The **Interactive Map** lights up with a blue vector polygon over the water candidate area.
   - Clicking the map polygon reveals the Popup with Evidence ID (`E-WATER-001`).
4. **Safe Claims to Make**:
   - *"NDWI $\ge 0.0$ identifies a potential water candidate. We do not overclaim 'confirmed flooding' without multi-sensor verification."*

---

### Step 5: Demonstrate Bi-Temporal Change Detection (3:00 – 3:45)
1. Click **`📅 Temporal Change — What changed?`** (or enter: `"Show spectral changes between the 2023 and 2024 Sentinel-2 observations."`).
2. Click **`▶ Execute Query`**.
3. **What the Judge Sees**:
   - `TemporalChangeSpecialist` processes $T_1$ and $T_2$ observations.
   - Map displays orange change candidate polygons.
   - The **Execution Trace** displays step `[1] T1: temporal_analysis — ✅ PASS` and step `[2] T2: verification — ✅ PASS`.
4. **Safe Claims to Make**:
   - *"Bialgebraic spectral rationing identifies candidate change areas."*

---

### Step 6: Demonstrate Multimodal Optical + SAR Reasoning (3:45 – 4:30)
1. Enter query: `"Analyze the area using both optical and SAR evidence."`
2. Click **`▶ Execute Query`**.
3. **What the Judge Sees**:
   - `MultimodalSpecialist` fuses Sentinel-2 MSI optical reflectance with Sentinel-1 C-SAR backscatter values.
   - Evidence cards display both `sensor: MSI` and `sensor: C-SAR`.
4. **Safe Claims to Make**:
   - *"We perform deterministic optical + SAR evidence fusion to cross-verify land surface observations."*

---

### Step 7: Demonstrate Hero Geographic Query (4:30 – 5:45)
1. Click **`🏗️ Hero Reasoning — Buildings near floods`** (or enter: `"Find newly constructed buildings within 500 m of flooded areas."`).
2. Click **`▶ Execute Query`** (or click `📄 hero_reasoning_e2e_audit.json` under Audit Replay for instant visualization).
3. **What the Judge Sees**:
   - **Task Plan**: Deconstructs into 6 steps (Water Candidate Detection $\rightarrow$ Building Candidate Detection $\rightarrow$ Temporal Change Analysis $\rightarrow$ 500m Buffer Creation $\rightarrow$ Spatial Intersection $\rightarrow$ Claim Verification).
   - **Interactive Map**: Shows 500m buffer zone (purple) and candidate building polygons (red) intersecting the buffer.
   - **Visual Evidence Panel**: Renders `hero_reasoning_overlay.png`.
   - **Confidence & Calibration Panel**: Shows progress bar with explicit warning: *"⚠️ Uncalibrated confidence. Values are raw model/rule outputs, not calibrated posterior probabilities."*
   - **Report Download**: Click `📥 Download JSON Report` to download the R13-compliant audit JSON.
4. **Safe Claims to Make**:
   - *"The system decomposes complex natural language into spatial primitives, computes a 500m GIS buffer around water candidates, intersects candidate building polygons, and verifies all evidence rules."*

---

### Step 8: Wrap-up & QA (5:45 – 6:30)
- Highlight the **Downloadable JSON Report** feature (SIH Requirement R13).
- Emphasize scientific honesty: uncalibrated confidence warning, candidate terminology, zero fabricated evidence.
- Invite judge questions.
