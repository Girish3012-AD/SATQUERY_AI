# SATQuery AI — Limitations & Scientific Claim Boundaries

This document explicitly defines the scientific claim boundaries, operational assumptions, and technical limitations of SATQuery AI to ensure scientific honesty and transparency during evaluation.

---

## 🔬 1. Scientific Claim Boundaries

### A. NDWI Water Candidate Detection vs. Confirmed Flooding
- **Claim Boundary**: The `WaterSpecialist` uses Normalized Difference Water Index ($\text{NDWI} \ge 0.0$) on Sentinel-2 optical bands (B03 Green, B08 NIR).
- **Strict Wording**: Results are emitted as **`potential_water_candidate`** or **`water_candidate`**.
- **Prohibited Claim**: The system does NOT claim that an NDWI threshold alone represents "confirmed flooding" or "disaster inundation" without multi-temporal baseline subtraction and hydrological validation.

### B. Spectral Change vs. Confirmed Building Construction
- **Claim Boundary**: The `TemporalChangeSpecialist` computes bi-temporal spectral difference between two observation dates ($T_1$ and $T_2$).
- **Strict Wording**: Results are emitted as **`candidate_change_area`** or **`spectral_change_candidate`**.
- **Prohibited Claim**: Spectral change alone does NOT prove building construction. Seasonal vegetation changes, agricultural harvesting, soil moisture variations, or cloud shadows can produce spectral differences. High-resolution optical imagery or CAD/LIDAR verification is required for structural confirmation.

### C. Spatial Proximity vs. Causal Relationship
- **Claim Boundary**: The hero geographic reasoning pipeline computes a 500 m spatial buffer around water candidates and intersects building candidate polygons.
- **Strict Wording**: Results establish **`spatial_association`** (features co-located within 500 meters).
- **Prohibited Claim**: Proximity does NOT prove causation or flood damage. The system does not claim that a building within 500 m of a water candidate was damaged by or caused a flood.

### D. Vision-Language VQA Interpretations
- **Claim Boundary**: VQA answers are generated by `Qwen2-VL-2B-Instruct` (fine-tuned with LoRA).
- **Strict Wording**: VQA answers are **model-generated textual hypotheses**.
- **Prohibited Claim**: VQA text is NOT a substitute for vector building detection or formal land-survey documentation. Small objects or low-resolution features may be hallucinated or omitted by the vision-language model.

### E. Confidence Scores & Calibration
- **Claim Boundary**: Confidence scores emitted by specialists (e.g. `confidence: 0.85`) are raw model activations or heuristic rule scores.
- **Strict Wording**: Scores are **`uncalibrated_raw_score`**.
- **Prohibited Claim**: Raw confidence scores must NOT be interpreted as true posterior probabilities or statistical certainty. The UI explicitly presents an uncalibrated disclaimer: *"⚠️ Uncalibrated confidence. Values are raw model/rule outputs, not calibrated posterior probabilities."*

## 🛠️ 2. Architectural & Technical Limitations

### 1. Optical/SAR Multimodal Fusion Implementation
- **Current Capability**: Multimodal reasoning uses **deterministic evidence-level fusion** (overlaying Sentinel-2 optical NDWI polygons with Sentinel-1 SAR VV backscatter intensity $\sigma^0$).
- **Limitation**: It does NOT currently employ a single end-to-end learned multimodal foundation transformer trained joint on optical + SAR tensors.

### 2. Bi-Temporal Imagery Requirements
- **Current Capability**: Co-registers $T_1$ and $T_2$ GeoTIFF rasters to `EPSG:32645`.
- **Limitation**: Requires input imagery with accurate spatial alignment and comparable cloud cover. Excessive cloud cover in $T_1$ or $T_2$ will degrade change detection accuracy.

### 3. VQA Inference Latency on CPU
- **Current Capability**: Runs `Qwen2-VL-2B-Instruct` in `float32` / `bfloat16` mode.
- **Limitation**: On CPU-only evaluation hardware, VQA model inference takes $\sim 2$ minutes per image. For live demonstration without GPU acceleration, the Audit Replay mode (`📂 AUDIT REPLAY`) is provided for instant judge visualization.

### 4. Offline Dataset Test Skips (`DATASET_REQUIRED`)
- **Current Capability**: The core codebase passes 100% of unit and integration tests (269 passed).
- **Limitation**: 24 training/dataset tests require multi-gigabyte raw SpaceNet4 and RS-VQA datasets. These tests use `pytest.mark.skipif` and are cleanly reported as `SKIPPED (DATASET_REQUIRED)` when dataset files are absent, avoiding false passes or repository bloat.
